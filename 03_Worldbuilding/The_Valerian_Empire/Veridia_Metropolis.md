# Veridia (The Three-Tiered Vertical Metropolis)

* **Physical Geography:** A colossal, vertical limestone city of 500,000 citizens built into and around a 300-foot sheer limestone bluff rising above the **River Aethel**.

```
╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║                                 THE THREE TIERS OF VERIDIA                                           ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║ TIER 1: THE HIGH CITADEL (The Crown & The Scriptorium)                                               ║
║ • Atop the 300-foot limestone bluff; enclosed by a 50-foot outer curtain wall.                       ║
║ • Landmarks: The Imperial Palace, The Great Scriptorium, and the Sacred Wellhead (The Weeping Deep). ║
║ • Controlled by: The King, [[01_Characters/Antagonists/Chancellor_Hector_Vance|Chancellor Vance]],  ║
║   and [[01_Characters/Antagonists/Inquisitor_Valerius_Crane|Inquisitor Crane]].                      ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║ TIER 2: THE MIDDLE CITY & WHARVES (The Mortal Sprawl)                                                ║
║ • Paved avenues, banking houses, artisan guilds, and dockland wharves along the River Aethel.        ║
║ • Landmarks: The Mavaros Townhouse & The Imperial Assay & Bullion House.                             ║
║ • Controlled by: [[01_Characters/Leads/Kyrell_Mavaros|Kyrell Mavaros]] & High Merchant Guilds.       ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║ TIER 3: THE UNDERCITY & CATACOMBS (The Sinks — Kyrell's Shadow Realm)                                ║
║ • Ancient vaulted Romanesque crypts, dry chambers, and flooded subterranean aqueduct canals.         ║
║ • Landmarks: The Black Cistern (Kyrell's private manor), The Ink Vaults, Rogue Scriptoriums,         ║
║   and The Ghost Tunnels connecting to noble cellars.                                                ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```
