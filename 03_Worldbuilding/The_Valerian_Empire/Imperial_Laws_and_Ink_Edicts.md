# Imperial Laws & Lex Scripta

---

## 1. The Ink Edicts (*Lex Scripta*)

* **The Crime of "Loose Pitch" (High Treason):** Possessing even a thimble of un-licensed magical ink or raw pitch stone is high treason, punishable by **Public Flaying** (excising the magical dermis with heated silver scrapers to shatter the circuit before execution).
* **The Master Registry Ledger:** Housed in Tier 1’s Great Scriptorium. Records the birth name, blood lineage, assigned discipline, and precise geometry of every legally inked subject. Constables audit active tattoos against rolled ledger chits at city gates.
* **Compulsory Vitality Conscription:** All bearers of Discipline 4 (Vitality/Healing) are classified as state strategic assets from the moment their glyph sets.

---

## 2. Internal Border & Security Edicts

* **Four-Duchy Transit Passport:** Commoners and merchants cannot cross ducal borders without a parchment warrant sealed in wax by a reigning Duke or magistrate.
* **Cargo Manifest Audits:** Freight barges and wagons must declare weight and barrel counts at river locks along the River Aethel.
* **The Night Curfew (The River Bells):** At the ninth toll of the Cathedral bell, heavy iron portcullises drop between Tier 1, Tier 2, and the wharves.

---

## 3. Sumptuary Laws & Blade Restrictions

* **The Blade Measure:** Civilian blades in Tier 1 and Tier 2 cannot exceed 18 inches. Broadswords and plate armor are restricted to imperial knights. Gentlemen may carry dress stilettos or concealed sword-canes (such as [[01_Characters/Leads/Kyrell_Mavaros|Kyrell]]'s ebony lynx cane).
* **Fabric Restrictions:** Crimson-dyed velvet, silk, and ermine fur are restricted to high nobility.
