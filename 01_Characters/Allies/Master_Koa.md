## 0. Backstory & Role in the Trilogy

* **30 Years Ago:** Commissioned by the Council of Elders as the greatest navigator of his generation to cross the Abyssal Reaches. Discovered the Valerian Empire, rescued Adira’s mother on the Port Morwen wharves, and brought her home to safety.
* **12 Years Ago:** Secretly sailed Adira’s mother back to the mainland to destroy her old records. Ambushed at the tide-line by Duke Thalor’s knights; fought to allow her to escape into the surf.
* **12 Years of Captivity:** Captured alive by Duke Thalor and locked in the flooded sea-dungeons of Port Morwen. For twelve years, he has resisted brutal interrogation, refusing to give Thalor the celestial wind-bearings needed to cross the Abyssal Gyre.
* **The Book 1 Rescue:** Freed by Adira and Kyrell during the raid on Port Morwen. His survival proves to Adira that her mother had a secret past and that Duke Thalor is preparing a private invasion armada!
