## 0. Canonical Profile & Motivation

* **Physical Condition:** **Completely Blank Flesh.** Tested as a youth, his body rejected magic. He carries a lifelong, bitter insecurity against inked warriors.
* **Public Role:** Chief Minister to the ailing King, civilian overseer of the Great Scriptorium, and commander of the Inquisitorial bureaucracy.
* **The Web of Lies:** 
  * Lied to the King 25 years ago about what caused [[01_Characters/Antagonists/Prince_Corin_Valerius|Prince Corin's]] coma.
  * Lied to the King 15 years ago claiming Corin died in his sleep.
  * Covered up the Batch II child experiment that created [[01_Characters/Leads/Kyrell_Mavaros|Kyrell Mavaros]].
* **Core Motivation:** Driven by a clinical obsession to capture Kyrell alive and dissect him on an anatomical slab to discover the biological mechanism of multi-grammar ink acceptance.
