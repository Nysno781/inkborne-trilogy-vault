## 0. Canonical Profile & Function

* **Governance:** Controls the western coastline where [[01_Characters/Leads/Adira_Solari|Adira]] and [[01_Characters/Allies/Ronan|Ronan]] wash ashore.
* **Corrupt Enterprise:** Secretly skims Crown tariffs and trades off-book timber with [[01_Characters/Leads/Kyrell_Mavaros|Kyrell]]'s river syndicate, unaware that Kyrell holds the incriminating ledgers that could have him executed for high treason.
* **The Conflict:** Plunders shipwrecks along the coastal reefs, attempting to capture Adira's vessel and her artifacts before the Crown learns of them.
