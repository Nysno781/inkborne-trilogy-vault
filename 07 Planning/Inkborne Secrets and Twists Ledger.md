# The Inkborne Trilogy — Secrets & Twists Ledger

> **Purpose:** one place that tracks *every* secret/twist in the series: what it is, who knows it, which book reveals it, and what setup it needs. This is the control panel that stops "a thousand directions" — a twist either has a row here with a reveal book, or it's discarded.
> **POV rule (locked):** single POV, Adira. The reader only ever learns what Adira learns. Secrets Kyrell holds are **subtext only** until she discovers them.

---

## Core locked rules that shape everything
1. Islanders (except High Elders) don't know the mainland exists. Commoners incl. Adira & Ronan believed they were alone under the sun.
2. Betrayals come only from the mainland hierarchy (Thalor, Vance, Corin) — the island foursome is unbreakable.
3. The isles are threatened by invasion (armada), never destroyed.
4. Kyrell is 100% self-made. **Lucan** is the secret royal bastard (Decoy Prince).

## Book 1 pacing fix — Thalor builds like a storm (T-threads)
- **T1 (Act I):** the shore is his Duchy; shipyards building too many hulls (her sailor's eye files it).
- **T2 (Act II):** her captor does business with "the Sea Duke"; his men joke about the Duke's paranoia.
- **T3 (Act III):** ledgers/manifests → ghost timber + naval stores → she cracks his case (her agency, his dead ends ending).
- **T4 (Act III):** the captive-wayfinder rumor reaches the Sinks → the party is pointed west.
- **T5 (Act IV):** all threads converge → the raid feels earned, not bolted-on.
