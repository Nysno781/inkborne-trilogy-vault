# ACT I — THE SHORE (Ch 1–5)

## Ch1 — The Morning of Departure
- Isle of Solara, dawn. Adira's last morning before the betrothal voyage to Korvath. Her father the High Clan Leader, Ronan, the handmaiden, five guards. The weight of duty — accepted, shouldered. Adira reads the sky and swells with easy mastery; she is in her element, whole and loved.
- Mid-chapter: the storm comes up faster than any stanza of the Song predicts. Adira at the helm fights to read a sea that has turned wrong. The ship breaks. Chaos, cold, blackness.

## Ch2 — Wake on the Sand (late afternoon)
- ✅ Adira wakes to find **Ronan already pulling the handmaiden from the waterline.** ✅ Late afternoon — no time to do anything but scramble above the tide line.
- The count: five guards missing. Inventory of the living: three. Grief and survivor's guilt hit her before pain.
- First night blind in a rock shelter / sea-cave — no fire (everything soaked), no food, no horizon. Adira lies flat and reads the swell through her spine: **the water is wrong.** She says nothing.

## Ch3 — Day 1: The Rite
- ✅ Dawn: the guard-captain's body washes in — the only one the sea gives back. 🔶 They hold vigil through the day and perform the improvised **ocean entombment at sunset**: torn sailcloth for white sea-silk, mainland stones for obsidian sinkers, no known deep channel. Adira speaks the sending stanzas; the handmaiden sings the mourning stanza; Ronan carries him out. The wrongness is felt by all three.
- Inventory (locked lean, kept lean): Adira's **obsidian dagger** (thigh-strap), Ronan's shark-tooth knife + the shaft of his sea-spear, a soaked fire-striker, one water cask, torn sailcloth, rope. No trousseau washes up.
- 🔶 Small addition: Ronan took a gash in the wreck — he hides how much it's hurting.

## Ch4 — Days 2–4: The Wrongness Catalogue
- They wait two days for rescue outriggers (island search custom). None come. They begin to scout.
- **Ronan** (sailor's eye): no reef ring, fish species he's never seen, driftwood from trees that don't grow on any isle, tides on the wrong schedule.
- **The handmaiden** (domestic eye): a log cut too clean (metal tool), a shard of pottery with a glaze no island kiln makes, a worn path heading inland.
- **Adira** (the Song in her head): climbs the headland — land to every horizon, no ring of sea, no caldera haze. She tries to place this coast in the Song and **every match fails.** She privately begins to count drift distance — and stops when the number gets too big to voice.
- When Ronan asks "which isle is this?" she lies — Mirela's far shore, we'll find smoke tomorrow. They believe her because they need to. The seed plants in silence.

## Ch5 — Days 5–6: The People
- Hunger forces the risk. From cover they watch charcoal-burners at a clearing's edge: pale skin, heavy wool, alien tools — and the **marks**. A man rolls his sleeve; dark geometric lines under the skin. A word falls from a mouth they can almost parse: *"ink."*
- The break: this is **not one of the Five Isles.** An entire land that should not exist. "We are not alone under the sun." (🔓 exactly when each of the three says it aloud — Ronan blunt, handmaiden in a whisper, Adira last.)
- New terror: bare skin makes them aliens here. And Ronan's hidden gash is festering; he needs medicine.
- They watch the settlement long enough to learn the waterfront has a merchant's staging-house — boats, stores, medicines. Desperation → the plan to steal, one night.
- 🌱 **T1 — Thalor builds:** Adira, a sailor, notices the shipyards are laying down **too many hulls, too large**, for the trade she can see. She files it without understanding it. (She is in the Sea Duke's duchy and doesn't know it yet.)

# ACT II — THE DEBT (Ch 6–13)

## Ch6 — The Theft & the Escape
- ✅ One desperate night, three bare-skinned foreigners slip the warehouse. They take: medicine, food, plain outer clothes (to cover their skin), a small boat. ✅ **One of Kyrell's men is injured** in the dark (not killed). The escape is clean — they vanish into the night and push off toward open sea.
- **The locket:** in the scramble, the cord snaps against a crate edge. Adira doesn't feel it go. Mid-row, when the fear eases, her hand goes to her throat — **nothing.** The bottom drops out. ✅ She realizes within the day — but Ronan is fevering and they cannot go back. She buries the grief. (Only she knows what was inside.)
- Small cruel irony they don't know: the "merchant" whose house they robbed is the Shadow Sovereign of the whole coast.

## Ch7 — The Sea Refuses
- Dawn on open water. Adira sets a course for home by stars and swell — and reads the current: **the Abyssal Gyre.** The sea runs one way; against the wind-shear and current their small boat cannot cross back. The way home is closed. (Heartbreak — the sea that raised her will not carry her home.)
- She gives Ronan and the handmaiden the partial truth (too strong, we need land, we'll find another way). Only she holds the full dread.
- They beach the boat (identifiable) and flee inland on foot. ✅ *The sea is hers, but not the land.*

## Ch8 — The Pursuit
- Through Adira's eyes: men asking questions at farmsteads and taverns — *three strangers, a girl and two men, no marks.* A price on information. The net is wider than she imagined. (We never see who's casting it.)
- They slip **one** roadblock — a small victory, reader breathes. Cost: Ronan collapses; the fever has him. They must carry him now. Medicine stolen isn't enough — he needs a real healer.

## Ch9 — The Capture & the Confrontation
- Cornered at last. The order Adira overhears chills her: **"Alive. He wants them alive."**
- ✅ The confrontation (locked beats): a room of hard men who want blood. The long silence. Adira reads him before he speaks: gloved hands, stillness, patience — he watches her the way she watches the sea. She is a wayfinder in a box with no sky.
- She speaks first — asks if Ronan lives, then offers herself to spare the others. First genuine surprise on his face: the leader is the bare-skinned girl.
- ✅ He publicly withholds punishment — turns their crime into proof of his control. His men obey instantly when he overrules them. ✅ He claims them as **debt, unspecified.** Not death. Not freedom.
- 🔶 One beat: when she looks him full in the face, something flickers under his collar and he goes very still. She files it; she doesn't understand it.
- (The missing locket aches through the whole scene like a pulled tooth.)

## Ch10 — The Terms & the Household
- ✅ Not prisoners — debtors, put to work. 🔶 To Adira's shock, Kyrell actually sends Ronan to his physician — she cannot reconcile it. Ronan will work guard/labor once healed. The handmaiden — Sera (name locked) — is set to a safe household post (her full identity still open).
- **Adira is kept near Kyrell** — assigned to the counting/manifest work of his household. She waits for the trap, sure he's planning something worse. She learns the shape of his domain, hates it, and hates that it's clean and orderly.
- She realizes the locket is truly gone (no chance to search the warehouse). Grief + a new terror: the drop is loose in the world. She cannot act.

## Ch11 — Track B Ignites (invisible to her)
- (No POV cut — his private world surfaces only as behavior.)
- Adira notices he watches her — her throat, her hands, the way she moves. Disturbed, she can't explain it. (Reader wonders with her — single POV.)
- **Lucan** enters: teasing, warm, calls her "the bare-skinned wonder." She dislikes and secretly likes him. Through him the household breathes.
- 🌱 **Vance as shadow:** a sealed letter arrives; even Lucan's grin thins. Adira overhears a name she'll learn to fear — *Vance.* The men go quiet. She stores it.

## Ch12 — The First Task
- He tests her. A task only she can do: she is **unmarked** — invisible to the Inquisition's ink-sense. (Crane enters the story as a name and a terror: purges sweeping the coast, posters, checkpoints.)
- 🔶 Concrete task: carry a sealed pouch past a checkpoint where Crane's men are stopping inked travelers — his people can't pass; she can. She does it on calm and nerve and her eye for detail. It goes slightly wrong; she improvises and slips through.
- She returns. He doesn't praise her — just watches, and she's furious that she wanted him to.
- (His private read, invisible: not just useful — *remarkable.* The debt ledger grows a second column he doesn't show her.)

## Ch13 — The Road to Veridia
- Kyrell concludes his coastal business and takes the household (debtors included) up the River Aethel toward Veridia. Forced proximity without pressure: deck work, night watches, shared silences.
- Adira glimpses the man behind the gloves: he has a wounded smuggler treated without ceremony; at a river stop, street orphans cling to his coat and he lets them; his men obey from loyalty, not fear. Her picture of the monster cracks.
- **The first tell she names:** she says his name plainly — *Kyrell* — and something flickers under his collar; he goes very still. Nothing mystical — she simply notices, the way you notice any man's composure slip — and files it like a landmark.
- 🔶 Lucan + Ronan banter (the duo comes alive); a quiet scene where the handmaiden and Adira speak of home.
- Arrival at the edge of Veridia — Tier 3, The Sinks, waits below. (End of Act II; hook into Act III.)
