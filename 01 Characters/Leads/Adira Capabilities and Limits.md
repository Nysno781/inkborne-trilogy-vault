# Adira Solari — Capabilities & Limits (Reference Sheet)

> Living reference. Built strictly from the vault + the four source docs. **No magic** — every capability is training, memory, and perception.
> Updated 2026-09-08.

---

## 1. Who she is

Master Wayfinder of the **Isle of Solara**, daughter of the High Clan Leader, age 23. She didn't choose her path — the betrothal and the station were chosen for her — but she **fully accepted and mastered it** (the Violet model). She was sailing to her betrothed's clan per tradition when the storm hit. On the isles she is celebrated; bare skin is the norm and "blank/lesser" is not a concept.

## 2. How her skill works (the key fact)

**Wayfinding is not magic.** It is a learned, elite discipline of **perception and memory**:

- The **Song of the Star Compass** — a massive oral epic (hundreds of sung stanzas) encoding stars, swells, currents, seasons, and coastlines. No paper charts, ever.
- **Bodily sensitivity** — reading pressure, temperature, and swell through bare soles and the spine while lying on a hull.
- **Celestial + atmospheric literacy** — stars, cloud-undersides ("cloud-mirror"), barometric shifts, wave-reflection pulses.

She's the best at it because she memorized the whole Song and has practiced since childhood — not because she was born with a gift no one else has.

## 4. Hard limits (what she CANNOT do)

- **No magic.** No ink, no glyphs, no supernatural anything. On the mainland this makes her *invisible to Crane's ink-sense* (an asset) AND read as "blank/nobody" (an injury to refuse).
- **No healing.** No vitality glyph — wounds, fever, poison are real threats. (Why the handmaiden-herbalist and Kyrell's physician are load-bearing; why Ronan's festering wound is a real clock.)
- **Helpless on land, at first.** Her genius is tuned to water/wind/current. On the mainland she is stripped of her domain — the locked theme **"the sea is hers, but not the land."**
- **Competence ≠ control.** She can read a sea and still lose a ship to it (Ch1). Skill doesn't guarantee outcome — that's what makes the storm tragic rather than convenient.

## 5. The two transfers (story engine)

1. **Oceanic → Land.** Her arc is turning a sea-genius loose on land: reading timber manifests and ship drafts like currents (cracking Thalor's ghost-timber case — her agency, his dead ends ending).
2. **The symmetry with Kyrell.** He is the most *controlled* man alive (8 glyphs, masks, gloves, unreadable). She is the one person the ink-system never touched — and, as she falls for him, the one who pays attention to what it did to him. *He hides behind the very system that marked him; she sees the man behind it.* No special mechanic — ordinary attentiveness is the whole point.

## 7. Note on the opening chapters

- Ch1–2 use the "wrongness in the water" (an absent deep current) as the wayfinder's *signature* of disaster — this is distinct from the (open) Gyre mechanism and stays consistent regardless of what we choose later.
