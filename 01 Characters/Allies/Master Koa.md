## 0. Backstory & Role in the Trilogy

* **30 Years Ago:** Commissioned by the Council of Elders as the greatest navigator of his generation to cross the Abyssal Reaches. Discovered the Valerian Empire, rescued Adira’s mother on the Port Morwen wharves, and brought her home to safety.
* **19 Years Ago (Adira was four):** Secretly sailed Adira's mother back to the mainland to destroy her old records. Ambushed at the tide-line by Duke Thalor's knights; fought to allow her to escape into the surf. She vanished into the western seas — and Adira was raised believing her mother had died.
* **19 Years of Captivity:** Captured alive by Duke Thalor and locked in the flooded sea-dungeons of Port Morwen. For nineteen years he has resisted brutal interrogation — and **he never broke**. Thalor holds his captured outrigger, but not the celestial wind-bearings: the armada has hulls, but no heading.
* **The Book 1 Rescue:** Freed by Adira and Kyrell during the raid on Port Morwen. His survival proves to Adira that her mother had a secret mainland past — and that Duke Thalor is building a private invasion armada. (Open: whether Koa knows her mother's true fate — see the Secrets & Twists Ledger.)
