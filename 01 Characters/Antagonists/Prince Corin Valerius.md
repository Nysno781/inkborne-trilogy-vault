## 0. Backstory & 25-Year Chronological Architecture

* **25 Years Ago (The Secret Pact):** The King’s firstborn bastard son approached [[01 Characters/Antagonists/Chancellor Hector Vance|Chancellor Vance]] in secret, volunteering for Batch I to gain absolute power. His nervous system overloaded into a catastrophic ink-fever coma.
* **The King's Intervention & Vance's 1st Lie:** Vance wanted to euthanize him. Vance lied to the King saying Corin had a sudden mysterious brain fever; the King ordered Corin placed in an alchemical cold-stasis crypt in the northern mountains. Vance believed Corin was a brain-dead vegetable who would die in his sleep.
* **15 Years Ago (The Mad Awakening & Vance's 2nd Lie):** After 10 years in stasis, Corin adapted, awoke in a psychopathic fury, slaughtered the crypt guards, and fled beyond the Glacial Wall. Vance lied to the King that Corin had died peacefully in his sleep.
* **15 Years to Present (The Frost Warlord):** Corin conquered the northern frost outcasts, building *The Ashen Host* and surviving on raw pitch. He now returns as his father the King lies dying, seeking [[03 Worldbuilding/The Sun-Gilded Isles/Mount Kora and Mountain Blood|Mount Kora's pure Mountain Blood]] to heal his neural deterioration.

## 1. Magic System & Inked Anatomy

* **Total Glyphs Held:** **10 Prime Glyphs** (Kinetic + Beast Summoning + Weaponized Sensory/Dread).
* **Fighting Style:** Euphoric, theatrical violence. Hums classical waltzes while firing bone-shattering kinetic shockwaves and commanding his Great Shadow-Wolf. Laughs through physical pain.

* **With [[01 Characters/Antagonists/Chancellor Hector Vance|Chancellor Vance]]:** Mocks Vance's 25 years of cowardice; Corin's return shatters Vance's political web of lies instantly.

* **With [[01 Characters/Leads/Adira Solari|Adira Solari]]:** Seeks her wayfinding skills and the Mountain Blood in her locket to achieve eternal, painless physical godhood.
