## 0. Basic Backstory

* The King's firstborn bastard son — a young adult when he volunteered for the Batch I experiments. His nervous system overloaded into catastrophic ink-fever; he was interred in an alchemical cold-stasis crypt in the northern mountains, awoke ten years later in a psychopathic fury, slaughtered the crypt guards, and fled beyond the Glacial Wall.
* **Age math (locked):** an adult at the secret pact 25 years ago; ten stasis years without aging leave him physically ~40.
* Everything else — where he has been, what he wants, who (if anyone) follows him, whether and when he returns — is **OPEN**.

## 1. Magic

* **Total Glyphs Held:** **10 Prime Glyphs** (Kinetic + Beast Summoning + Weaponized Sensory/Dread) — the only stasis-adapted multi-discipline bearer on record.
* **Fighting Style:** Euphoric, theatrical violence. Hums classical waltzes mid-carnage; laughs through pain.
