## 0. Canonical Profile & Function

* **Function:** Tactical military commander of the Inquisitorial Order; leads the purges against unlicensed ink across the Four Duchies.
* **Personality:** Fanatical, ascetic, and brutally efficient. Views all street-cut ink and black-market pitch as an existential disease.
* **Role in Book 1:** The purge commander sweeping the coast — checkpoints, posters, and unlicensed-ink purges through Port Morwen and the Veridian wharves. He hunts Kyrell's smuggling networks throughout; the manhunt for the thieves themselves is run by Kyrell's own network until Act IV's counter-hunt, when Crane's net closes on the survivors at the water's edge. Adira, unmarked, is invisible to his ink-sense — she moves through his blind spots. (Open: his end-of-book fate — see the Secrets & Twists Ledger.)
