## 0. Canonical Profile & Function

* **Function:** Tactical military commander of the Inquisitorial Order; leads the purges against unlicensed ink across the Four Duchies.
* **Personality:** Fanatical, ascetic, and brutally efficient. Views all street-cut ink and black-market pitch as an existential disease.
* **Role in Book 1:** The relentless hound tracking the shipwreck survivors ([[01 Characters/Leads/Adira Solari|Adira]] and [[01 Characters/Allies/Ronan|Ronan]]) and hunting [[01 Characters/Leads/Kyrell Mavaros|Kyrell]]'s smuggling networks through Port Morwen and the Veridian wharves.
