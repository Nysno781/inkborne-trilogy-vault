## 0. Canonical Profile & Function

* **Identity:** Eldest son and heir to the Clan Leader of the **Isle of Korvath**.
* **The Conflict with Adira:** Viewed the arranged marriage strictly as an instrument to bind Clan Solari's celestial wayfinders to his family's obsidian monopoly. Expected a docile, submissive clan wife and resented Adira’s independence and free spirit.
* **The Sentry Search:** Believing Adira fled their wedding intentionally, he mobilizes island search outriggers, threatening to destabilize the Council of Five.
