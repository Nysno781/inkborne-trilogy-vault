# Style Guide, Tone & Romance Rules

---

## 1. Aesthetic Core & Atmosphere
* **Aesthetic:** Dark Gothic Feudal Fantasy colliding with Lyrical Oceanic Naturalism.
* **Sensory Details:** 
  * Rain-slicked cobblestones, river fog frosting in the air, glowing blue-black dermis circuits, iron braziers burning pine coal, bitter black tea, crisp linen, tailored dark wool.
  * Contrast with warm salt-mist, phosphorescent lagoon currents, polished mother-of-pearl, and translucent volcanic obsidian.
* **Pacing:** Fast-paced, high-stakes tactical momentum balanced with atmospheric, slow-burn romantic tension.

---

## 2. Hard Romance Rules `[Confirmed Canon]`
* **No Insta-Love / No Insta-Lust:** Attraction is grounded in observation, mutual respect, and undeniable physical awareness. No immediate panting declarations or foolish surrender of boundaries.
* **Enemies-to-Allies-to-Lovers Trajectory:** Initial wariness and tactical suspicion transform through shared danger, competence, and vulnerability into fierce, unshakeable devotion.
* **Angst & Jealousy:** Deep emotional stakes, high-society ballroom friction, protective instincts, and the agonizing weight of duty versus personal desire.
* **Touch-Starvation & Micro-Tells:** 
  * Mainland culture treats bare skin as intensely private (gloves worn at all times).
  * The progressive removal of gloves, accidental brush of fingers over primed tattoos, and tending of wounds carry immense erotic and emotional charge.
  * Adira is the *only* person in the world who can read Kyrell’s subtle ink micro-tells beneath his collar and wrists.

---

## 3. Linguistic & Dialogue Conventions `[Confirmed Option 1]`
* **Universal Common Tongue:** Both mainlanders and islanders speak the same language (eliminating tedious translation drag and allowing immediate, sharp banter from Chapter 1).
* **Cadence & Dialect Contrast:**
  * **Mainlanders:** Formal, clipped, measured cadence. Rich in legal metaphors, assay terminology, and guarded court courtesy.
  * **Islanders:** Rhythmic, lyrical, direct. Rich in nautical idioms, celestial metaphors, and open honesty.
