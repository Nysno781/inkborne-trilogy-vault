# Inkborne — Style Spec & Voice Bible (v1)

> Locked from the author's own words + the compass. This governs every draft from here on.
> Status: **LOCKED unless the author revises.**

---

## 1. The voice, in one line

**First-person present, Adira only** — a free-spirited, witty, intelligent young woman who loves the sea and hasn't been broken yet, narrated with Fourth Wing's energy and banter but *slightly less modern*, and lyricism saved for the moments that earn it.

## 2. Adira's baseline voice — DO / DON'T

| DO | DON'T |
|---|---|
| Sharp, quick, sarcastic; banter as a reflex | Melancholy interiority; brooding |
| Physical, kinetic images (she lives in her body — diving, sailing, running) | Abstract/poetic navel-gazing as the default |
| Salt-and-sailing idiom ("the tide's against me") | Modern internet slang ("okay, that tracks", "absolutely not", "fair enough") |
| Joy in her element — the sea as playground and home | The sea as doom/omen in *every* sentence |
| Fresh, raw grief when the storm hits (it's her FIRST tragedy) | Pre-existing heaviness, world-weary sadness |
| Free spirit, unbound — impulsive, honest, open | Immature/flighty; she's grounded and competent when it counts |
| Intelligent observation (reads people, weather, a room) | Naivety that gets her in trouble on purpose |

**Illustrative shift (old draft → new voice):**
- OLD: *"The sea that raised me closed its hand around us, and I was the one person who should have known."*
- NEW: *"I've sailed this ring a hundred times. I've never once seen the water look at me like that."* — still the dread, but through *her* eyes: surprise, not self-flagellation.
- OLD (heavy): *"Grief is a cold thing. I know the shape of it."*
- NEW (free spirit, first loss): *"I don't know how to hold this. It doesn't fit anywhere in me yet."*

## 3. Register shifts (rhythm by scene type) — LOCKED

| Scene type | Rhythm | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Action / pursuit / theft / raid | **Lean & kinetic.** Short paragraphs, short sentences, high dialogue ratio, commentary during motion | Fourth Wing propulsion |
| The isles / the Song / departure / romance | **Lush & slow.** Longer lines, sensory abundance, room to breathe | ACOTAR immersion minus melodrama |
| Storm / wrong-water / locket-grief / romantic crests | **Earned lyricism.** The imagery gets turned up to full — this is the only place the prose may go "poetic" | Broadbent precision at the peaks |
| Everyday survival (the wrongness catalogue, safehouse days) | **Middle register.** Observant, wry, warm — free-spirit default | — |

## 4. Wit rules (Violet-minus-modern)

- Snark = fast, physical, self-aware. Banter is a sport she's *good* at.
- Idiom comes from the sea and the isles: tides, swells, currents, stars, caldera smoke, obsidian, wayfinding. Not from the internet.
- She's smart, not smug: her wit lands *with* people (Ronan, later Lucan), not over them.
- Under pressure, the wit clicks OFF and the Master Wayfinder clicks ON. The switch is the tell.

## 5. Romance register — LOCKED (charged banter + possessive-oath)

- **Friction-as-flirtation:** verbal sparring, mutual competence, neither yielding — attraction earned through *matching* each other, never insta-lust.
- **Possessive-oath, not verdict:** "she is under my mantle" (territorial *protection*), NOT "you belong to me" (ownership). Xaden/Rhys, not Tamlin.
- **He falls first, she falls harder** — his side shows only through actions (single POV); hers through the slow crack of trust.
- **Touch-starvation is the engine:** the almost-touch, wound-tending, the mantle-drape, the three-dance rule. Save the first true touch for the crest.
- **Her attentiveness to him** is the romance's secret weapon — she notices what no one else bothers to; deployed sparingly, each time landing harder.

## 6. Lyricism budget

Default register = witty and physical. Lyricism is a **tap, not a flood** — opened only at:
1. The storm (Ch1) — the water turning wrong.
2. The first night / "the water is wrong" (Ch2) — but through surprise, not doom.
3. The locket loss (Ch6) — grief sharp and new.
4. The isles in memory — the Song, the caldera, home.
5. The great romantic crests (Ch28+) — when restraint finally breaks.

## 7. What this means for the existing drafts

- **Ch1 & Ch2 need a voice retune:** keep the *events* (they're locked) and the *structure* (they work) — but re-voice Adira from solemn to free-spirited-witty. The storm and the wrong water stay heavy; everything else lightens, quickens, sharpens.

## 8. Cast dynamics notes (added 2026-09-08)

- **Sera (handmaiden):** competent, steady, unsentimental — the organizer/healer who carries Adira's old life. Grief shows as strength, not tears. NOT fragile. (Corrected from early drafts.)
- **Ronan's secret crush on Sera:** subtle, never overdone — a beat too long looking, an odd gentleness, Adira noticing without processing. Do not lampshade it.
- **Dialogue density:** interiority is not the default. Dialogue rises as the cast forms (Ronan/Sera banter → Lucan/crew → Kyrell/court). Ch1–3 = moderate; Ch9+ = banter-rich (this is where the charged-banter romance register lives).
