# Series Architecture & Story Pillars

---

## 1. Scope & Word Count Targets

* **Format:** Trilogy (3 Books).
* **Target Word Count per Book:** ~90,000 – 100,000 words (~30–33 chapters per book).
* **Total Series Scope:** ~270,000 – 300,000 words total.
* **Scope Discipline:** 
  * Each book resolves its own central tactical arc while escalating the broader romantic and political stakes.
  * Every major mystery has an airtight, pre-planned answer—no third-book debt or unplanned supernatural bloat.

## 3. The 3 Narrative Engines Model

Every act in the trilogy simultaneously advances three parallel engines:
1. **The External Engine:** Hard-magic ink warfare, smuggling logistics, inquisitorial purges, court intrigue, and military conquest.
2. **The Romantic Engine:** Enemies-to-allies-to-lovers slow burn, mutual competence, touch-starvation, ballroom jealousy, forced proximity, and soul-deep devotion.
3. **The Internal Engine:** Healing from childhood trauma (Kyrell's orphan scars / Adira's sacrifice of autonomy), shedding the core lie, and stepping into sovereign identity.
