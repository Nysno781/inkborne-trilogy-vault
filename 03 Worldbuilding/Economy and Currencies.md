# Economy, Coinage & The Ink Scrip Standard

---

## 1. The Imperial Three-Metal Standard (Mainland)

1. **Copper Marks:** Everyday peasant transactions (bread, ale, candles, common tools).
2. **Silver Shillings:** Merchant trade, lodging, horses, textiles, livestock.
3. **Gold Sovereigns / Crowns:** Noble real estate, grand contracts, state taxes.

---

## 2. The Underworld "Ink Scrip" Standard

* **The Problem:** Imperial gold coins are heavy, noisy, and seized at checkpoints.
* **The Solution:** Watermarked parchment promissory notes issued exclusively by [[01 Characters/Leads/Kyrell Mavaros|Kyrell Mavaros]] through the **Imperial Assay & Bullion House**.
* **The Backing:** Backed 1:1 by certified volumes of pure Prime Ink stored in the subterranean **Ink Vaults** of Tier 3. Functioning as the most trusted currency across the continental underworld.

---

## 3. The Island Barter Economy (The Ring of Five)

* **Pure Resource Exchange:** No minted coins, metal currency, or tax ledgers. The five islands trade through formal resource agreements (e.g., timber for obsidian, or fruit for sea-salt and pearls).
* **Clan Generosity:** Wealth is measured by family contribution, boat quality, and craftsmanship rather than hoarded gold.
