# The Four Provincial Duchies & The Solitary Empire

* **The Solitary Realm Worldview:** In mainland dogma and law, **The Valerian Empire is the sole civilization in existence**. Flanked by the uncharted **Abyssal Reaches** to the West, frozen glacial barrens to the North, and sheer dead canyons to the South.
* **The River Aethel:** The massive, fog-cloaked commercial artery running through the heart of the continent, controlled logistically by [[01 Characters/Allies/Lucan Cross|Lucan Cross]]'s Grand River Freight.
