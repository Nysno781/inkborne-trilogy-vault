# The Sun-Gilded Isles (The Ring of Five / The Pelagos)

* **Physical Geography:** A distant, uncharted volcanic archipelago consisting of **five major tropical islands** arranged in a natural ring around the active central volcanic caldera of [[03 Worldbuilding/The Sun-Gilded Isles/Mount Kora and Mountain Blood|Mount Kora]].
* **The Clan Lineage Rule (Model 2 Confirmed):** Clan names are exclusive honorifics held solely by the **hereditary Clan Leaders and their direct bloodline** (*Clan Solari*). Commoners go by single names or craft titles.
