# Island Culture, Wayfinding & Life-Cycle

---

## 1. Wayfinding & Celestial Navigation

* **The Song of the Star Compass:** Islanders use zero paper charts; navigation is taught as a massive oral epic poem of hundreds of sung stanzas.
* **Reading Swells by Touch:** Wayfinders lie on the hull of voyaging catamarans, sensing deep-ocean swell reflections and temperature shifts through their bare soles and spine.
* **Cloud-Mirror Sense:** Reading the pale-green underside of distant storm clouds reflecting hidden atolls dozens of miles away.

---

## 2. Hospitality & Customs

* **The "Obsidian Breath" (*Te Hā*):** Two allies press their foreheads and noses together, closing their eyes and taking a single shared breath to acknowledge mutual peace and no hidden blades.
* **The Open Palm:** Walking with open palms touching the heart and host's shoulder.
* **Communal Earth-Oven (*The Umu*):** Roasted tuna, taro, and breadfruit; the first ten percent of every catch is shared with elders and divers.

---

## 3. Life-Cycle & Rites

* **The Saltwater Immersion:** Newborn infants are gently dipped in the incoming ocean tide to welcome them as children of the sea.
* **The Unmarked Canvas:** Islanders oil their skin with coconut and hibiscus oil; smooth, completely unmarked golden-bronze skin is celebrated as the highest standard of health.
* **Ocean Entombment:** No graves on land; bodies are wrapped in white sea-silk weighted with obsidian sinkers and released into the deep abyssal channel at sunset.
