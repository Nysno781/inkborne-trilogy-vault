# Discipline 3: Beast Summoning & Constructs

* **Visual Family:** Open, asymmetric, curvilinear; a stroke always runs off the edge of the skin into negative space.
* **The One-Beast Rule:** A bearer binds only **ONE animal device permanently**. If destroyed in combat, that ink is permanently dead.
* **Construct Physics:** Every bearer anchors the **same fixed volume of ink mass**. Small animals (lynx, fox, owl) are steel-dense, fast, and strike like solid cannonballs; large animals (bear, stag, horse) trade density for reach and grappling mass.
* **Form & Senses Only:** Constructs replicate the animal's physical shape and acute senses (night vision, smell, hearing), but **zero animal chemistry** (no venom, no poisons, no toxins).

## The 4 Canonical Glyphs

### 1. Manifestation (The Sweeping Arc)
* **Visual Geometry:** Shared outer open arc containing the regional device sigil.
* **The 12 Regional Devices:**
  1. *Timber Wolf* (North • Pursuit & Pack)
  2. *Red Stag* (North • Shock Charge)
  3. *Brown Bear* (North • Door-Breaking Mass)
  4. *War Horse* (East • Speed & Mounted Impact)
  5. *Red Fox* (Any • Infiltration & Small Gaps)
  6. *Adder* (North • Confined Ambush)
  7. *Black Raven* (Any • Aerial Scouting)
  8. *Hunting Falcon* (Any • High-Speed Aerial Stoop)
  9. *Barn Owl* (Any • Silent Night Recon)
  10. *River Trout* (East • Submerged Scouting)
  11. *Wolverine* (North Mountains • Unyielding Ferocity)
  12. **Lynx** (North • Silent Ambush, Shadow Vision, Precision Throat Kill — *Held by [[01 Characters/Leads/Kyrell Mavaros|Kyrell Mavaros]]*).

### 2. Partial Manifestation (The Broken Arc)
* **Visual Geometry:** Sweeping curve interrupted by a clean gap with two inward-pointing spur strokes.
* **Placement Convention:** Forearm or hand.
* **Effect:** Manifests claws, fangs, or sensory traits onto human limbs without summoning the full construct.

### 3. Surface Spread (The Fan)
* **Visual Geometry:** One stroke splitting into four thin tapering lines.
* **Placement Convention:** Back or shoulders.
* **Effect:** Spreads ink across floors, walls, and water as a sensory tripwire or stealth manifestation zone.

### 4. Extended Reach (The Tether)
* **Visual Geometry:** Long curving line with three evenly spaced nodes.
* **Placement Convention:** Thigh or calf.
* **Effect:** Allows the construct to operate at long range across rooftops or city blocks away from the bearer.
