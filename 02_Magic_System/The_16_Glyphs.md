# The 16 Canonical Glyphs Grimoire

There are **27 fixed visual designs in total**: Discipline 3 (Beast Summoning)'s first slot carries **12 regional device variants**; the other 15 glyphs are single-form.

---

## The Four Visual Families

| Discipline | Visual Logic & Geometry | Field Identification Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **[[02_Magic_System/Disciplines/Discipline_1_Kinetic\|1. Kinetic & Physical]]** | Angular, straight-line trussed geometry; hard angles; self-bracing like gothic vault ribs. | *"If it looks like it is holding something up, it is a body mark."* |
| **[[02_Magic_System/Disciplines/Discipline_2_Sensory\|2. Mental & Sensory]]** | Radial, concentric, balanced around a central point; no direction. | *"If it looks like it is staring at you from its centre, it is a mind mark."* |
| **[[02_Magic_System/Disciplines/Discipline_3_Beast_Summoning\|3. Beast Summoning]]** | Open, asymmetric, curvilinear; a stroke always runs off the edge into negative space. | *"If it looks like a brushstroke of something running, it is a beast mark."* |
| **[[02_Magic_System/Disciplines/Discipline_4_Vitality\|4. Vitality & Healing]]** | Single continuous unbroken line folding through itself into knotwork; no endpoints. | *"If you cannot find where it starts, it is a healing mark."* |

## Master Catalog of All 16 Glyphs

### Discipline 1: Physical / Kinetic Defense
* **1. Strength (The Corbel):** Two angled strokes rising to meet a horizontal bar with a heavy junction node. (Forearm / shoulder).
* **2. Speed (The Chevron Stack):** Three nested chevrons decreasing in size like an arrowhead stack. (Calf / thigh — *held by Lucan & Kyrell*).
* **3. Kinetic Deflection Shield (The Keystone):** Tapered hexagonal plate bisected by a vertical stroke. (Chest / back / inner forearm — *held by Lucan & Kyrell*).
* **4. Point-Blank Kinetic Shock (The Wedge):** Heavy vertical stroke with two flaring angled spurs at the base. (The hand — *held by Kyrell beneath his gloves*).

### Discipline 2: Subtle / Sensory Manipulation
*(Hard rule: Strictly NO mind-reading and NO mind-control).*
* **1. Dread Aura (The Eye):** Heavy filled central node ringed by two concentric circles; radiates crushing atmospheric pressure. (Sternum / throat).
* **2. Perception (The Aperture):** Central node ringed once with four radial ticks at the quarters; calibrates focus and micro-tells. (Temple / wrist).
  * *Inquisitorial Variant:* Ticks doubled, center left hollow; calibrated to sense the resonance of nearby magical ink within 3 paces (*held by Inquisitor Crane*).
* **3. Mental Shielding (The Sealed Circle):** Two complete concentric rings with nothing at the center; blocks fear and sensory shock. (Forehead / chest).
* **4. Sensory Camouflage (The Dashed Ring):** Single ring broken into evenly spaced dashed arcs; dampens scent, sound, and visual presence. (Throat / back of neck).

### Discipline 3: Beast Summoning & Constructs
*(Hard rule: The One-Beast Rule — a bearer binds only ONE animal construct permanently).*
* **1. Manifestation (The Sweeping Arc):** Shared outer open frame containing the regional device sigil (12 variants: Lynx, Wolf, Stag, Bear, Horse, Fox, Adder, Raven, Falcon, Owl, Trout, Wolverine).
* **2. Partial Manifestation (The Broken Arc):** Sweeping curve with a clean gap and two inward-pointing spur strokes; manifests claws, fangs, or senses onto human body.
* **3. Surface Spread (The Fan):** One stroke splitting into four thin tapering lines; spreads ink across floors, walls, and water.
* **4. Extended Reach (The Tether):** Long curving line with three evenly spaced nodes; extends beast manifestation range across a city block.

### Discipline 4: Vitality & Healing
*(Hard rule: Cannot regrow severed limbs, cannot revive the dead).*
* **1. Sutures & Clotting (The Lattice):** Single closed line crossing four times into a square mesh; instantly seals deep hemorrhages. (Forearm / belly).
* **2. Pain Deadening (The Doubling Spiral):** Closed line spiraling inward and returning outward; dampens trauma signals. (Base of skull / spine).
* **3. Poison Neutralization (The Trefoil):** Closed line looping into three interlocked rings; filters venoms and toxins. (Inner elbow / thigh).
* **4. Bone-Setting (The Splint):** Closed line running as two parallel straights joined by zigzags; binds compound fractures into rigid alignment. (Shin / forearm).
