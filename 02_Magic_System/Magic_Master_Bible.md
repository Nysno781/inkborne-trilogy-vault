# Magic Master Bible — Universal Laws & The Cave

---

## 1. Core Foundational Premise
* **The Medium:** Magic comes strictly from **ink bound to human dermis**. Raw ink in a bottle or un-inked pitch stone is completely inert.
* **Era & Technology:** Timeless Feudal Dark Fantasy — stone keeps, torchlit catacombs, cold iron, swords, rapiers, bows, horseback travel. Strictly no firearms or modern machinery.

---

## 2. Origin of the Grammar — The Sacred Cave

* **Discovered, Not Invented:** The 16 geometric glyphs were discovered in an ancient subterranean cave beneath [[03_Worldbuilding/The_Valerian_Empire/Veridia_Metropolis|Veridia]], connected directly to the **Weeping Deep** wellhead.
* **Shapes Without Theory:** The Empire possesses the shapes and proportions, but zero theoretical understanding of *why* the nodes or circuits work.
* **The Archive, Not a Lab:** The Great Scriptorium in Tier 1 preserves copies of the original cave wall. Master Scriptors copy; they do not research, modify, or invent.
* **Copy Drift:** Centuries of manual copying have introduced subtle node errors; some legal inkings fail inexplicably due to copy drift.
* **Fixed Grammar:** There is no such thing as a "new glyph."

---

## 3. The Universal One-Discipline Law & Capacity Tiers

* **The Biological Law:** The human central nervous system and bloodstream can naturally anchor and harmonize with only **ONE single discipline**.
* **Setting & Binding:** A body binds ink as it matures; once the nervous system harmonizes around one discipline, the geometry permanently locks.
* **Incompatible Resonance / Rejection:** Inking a glyph from a second discipline onto an ordinary body causes violent rejection: grand mal seizures, black blood-rot, or fatal stroke.

### Capacity Tiers
| Tier | Glyphs Held | Examples |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ordinary Bearer** | 1 to 3 glyphs of one discipline | Common guards, street enforcers, [[01_Characters/Allies/Lucan_Cross|Lucan Cross]] (2 Kinetic glyphs) |

| **Prime Overload / Apex Warlord** | 10 Prime glyphs | [[01_Characters/Antagonists/Prince_Corin_Valerius|Prince Corin Valerius]] (10 glyphs across 3 disciplines via stasis adaptation) |

---

## 4. The Circuit Rule & Dermic Binding

* **The Geometry is the Circuit:** Ink is only the conductive medium. The circuit binds **only** during the original hand-poking into living dermis.
* **Excised Ink is Dead:** Severed skin cannot hold a charge. **Skin-grafting or tattoo transfers are biologically impossible.**
* **Destruction:** Deliberate flaying (with hot silver scrapers) or catastrophic third-degree burns permanently destroy the circuit. **A damaged glyph can never be repaired or re-inked.**
