# Circuits, Junction Faults & Biological Tolls

---

## 1. The Fuel & Metabolic Toll

* **Biological Energy & Oxygen:** Magic consumes calories, biological hydration, and blood-oxygen at an accelerated rate.
* **Per-Discipline Toll:** Channeling multiple glyphs within *one* discipline is sustainable (similar to high-intensity sprinting).
* **Dual-Discipline Overdrive:** Channeling two disciplines simultaneously doubles the metabolic and neurological load, causing rapid fatigue, severe dehydration, and junction inflammation if maintained.
* **Mortality:** Inked warriors possess standard human biology. [[01_Characters/Leads/Kyrell_Mavaros|Kyrell]] has zero healing magic—he bleeds, fractures bones, and suffers poisons like any mortal man.

## 3. Kyrell's 3 Junction Faults

Normal inking isolates glyphs with deliberate blank space. In the Batch II experiments, [[01_Characters/Antagonists/Chancellor_Hector_Vance|Vance]] tested **interweaving geometries** across disciplines. 

Kyrell carries **8 glyphs: 5 isolated, 3 interwoven junction points**. Junctions are **structural faults, not intentional powers**:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 1. FOREARM & HAND: Partial Manifestation (Beast) × Point-Blank Kinetic Shock (Kinetic)      │
│ • THE FAULT: A summoned lynx claw sometimes KINETICALLY DETONATES on impact instead of      │
│   cutting cleanly, pulverizing objects he intended to open delicately.                      │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 2. BACK & TORSO: Kinetic Deflection Shield (Kinetic) × Surface Spread (Beast)               │
│ • THE FAULT: When spreading ink across a floor or wall, calling a personal deflection       │
│   shield sometimes triggers at the SPREAD'S remote location instead of around his body,     │
│   leaving his torso completely undefended.                                                  │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 3. THIGH & CALF: Extended Reach (Beast) × Speed (Kinetic)                                   │
│ • THE FAULT: Sprinting at maximum kinetic speed while his lynx is at full extended range    │
│   sometimes SNAPS the tether back, violently materializing the beast directly on top of him.│
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

* **The Bloom Tell:** Under extreme physical exhaustion, these 3 junction points **bloom first** as dark, burning bruises before the rest of his ink surfaces.
* **The Only Reader:** [[01_Characters/Leads/Adira_Solari|Adira]] is the only person observant enough to read both his emotional micro-tells and his junction fatigue.
