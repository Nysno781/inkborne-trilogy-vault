# Discipline 2: Mental & Sensory Manipulation

* **Visual Family:** Radial, symmetric, centered; concentric circles balanced around a single central node.
* **Hard Canon Constraints:** Strictly **NO mind-reading, NO telepathy, and NO mind-control**. Neither may ever be added.

---

## The 4 Canonical Glyphs

### 1. Dread Aura (The Eye)
* **Visual Geometry:** Heavy filled central node surrounded by two concentric rings.
* **Placement Convention:** Sternum or throat.
* **Effect:** Projects an involuntary, suffocating aura of biological terror and paralysis within close range.

### 2. Perception (The Aperture)
* **Visual Geometry:** Central node ringed once, with four short radial ticks at the quarters.
* **Placement Convention:** Temple or wrist.
* **Effect:** Calibrates micro-focus, hearing, and physical reaction time.
* **The Inquisitorial Variant:** Ticks doubled, center left hollow. Applied exclusively to [[01_Characters/Antagonists/Inquisitor_Valerius_Crane|Inquisitor Valerius Crane]] and high inquisitors to sense dormant magical ink within 3 paces.

### 3. Mental Shielding (The Sealed Circle)
* **Visual Geometry:** Two complete concentric rings with **nothing at the center**.
* **Placement Convention:** Forehead or chest.
* **Effect:** Immunizes the bearer against intimidation, sensory overload, and external Dread Auras.

### 4. Sensory Camouflage (The Dashed Ring)
* **Visual Geometry:** Single ring broken into evenly spaced dashed arcs.
* **Placement Convention:** Throat, soles of feet, or back of neck.
* **Effect:** Dampens footstep acoustics, scent output, and visual presence in shadows.
