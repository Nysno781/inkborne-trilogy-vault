# Discipline 4: Vitality & Healing

* **Visual Family:** One continuous, unbroken line folding through itself into knotwork; no endpoints.
* **Hard Canon Constraints:** Cannot regrow severed limbs; cannot revive the dead. Stabilizes, seals, and accelerates natural cellular recovery.
* **State Conscription:** Because healers can reverse battlefield attrition, the Crown classifies all Vitality bearers as state property. Unlicensed healers are hunted or work strictly for [[01_Characters/Leads/Kyrell_Mavaros|Kyrell]]'s underworld vaults.

---

## The 4 Canonical Glyphs

### 1. Sutures & Clotting (The Lattice)
* **Visual Geometry:** Single closed line crossing four times into a square mesh.
* **Placement Convention:** Inner forearm or belly.
* **Effect:** Instantly halts arterial bleeding and binds lacerations.

### 2. Pain Deadening (The Doubling Spiral)
* **Visual Geometry:** Closed line spiraling inward and returning outward along its own path.
* **Placement Convention:** Base of skull or spine.
* **Effect:** Blocks extreme trauma and nerve pain signals.

### 3. Poison Neutralization (The Trefoil)
* **Visual Geometry:** Closed line looping through itself into three interlocked rings.
* **Placement Convention:** Inner elbow or thigh.
* **Effect:** Rapidly filters organic poisons and venoms from the bloodstream.

### 4. Bone-Setting (The Splint)
* **Visual Geometry:** Closed line running as two parallel straights joined at each end by a short zigzag.
* **Placement Convention:** Shin or forearm.
* **Effect:** Realizes instant structural alignment and rapid calcification of fractured bones.
