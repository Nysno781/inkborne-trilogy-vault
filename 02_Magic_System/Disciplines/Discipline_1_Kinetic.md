# Discipline 1: Physical Enhancement & Kinetic Defense

* **Visual Family:** Angular, straight-line trussed geometry; hard angles; self-bracing like gothic vault ribs.
* **Telltale Sensory Effect:** Compresses ambient air—candle flames bow away, loose papers flatten, and nearby observers feel a sudden ear-popping barometric drop.

---

## The 4 Canonical Glyphs

### 1. Strength (The Corbel)
* **Visual Geometry:** Two angled strokes rising to meet a short horizontal bar, with a single heavy node at the junction.
* **Placement Convention:** Forearm or shoulder.
* **Effect:** Multiplies muscular tensile output; allows crushing iron portcullises and overpowering heavy plate armor.

### 2. Speed (The Chevron Stack)
* **Visual Geometry:** Three nested chevrons decreasing in size, pointing in a single direction.
* **Placement Convention:** Calf or thigh.
* **Effect:** Burst propulsion and rapid directional redirection. (*Held by [[01_Characters/Allies/Lucan_Cross|Lucan Cross]] and [[01_Characters/Leads/Kyrell_Mavaros|Kyrell Mavaros]]*).

### 3. Kinetic Deflection Shield (The Keystone)
* **Visual Geometry:** Tapered hexagonal plate bisected by a single vertical stroke.
* **Placement Convention:** Chest, back, or inner forearm.
* **Effect:** Manifests a transparent plane of compressed kinetic force capable of deflecting crossbow bolts, thrown daggers, and heavy sword blows. (*Held by Lucan and Kyrell*).

### 4. Point-Blank Kinetic Shock (The Wedge)
* **Visual Geometry:** Heavy vertical stroke with two short angled spurs flaring outward at the base.
* **Placement Convention:** The palm / back of the hand.
* **Effect:** Releases an explosive, armor-shattering kinetic pulse at point-blank impact. (*Held by Kyrell beneath his bespoke leather gloves*).
